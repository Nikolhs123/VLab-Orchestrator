# Publication Preparation History

The VLab Orchestrator manuscript and software artifact were initially prepared
for SoftwareX. A draft submission was prepared, but the submission was not
completed because funding for the article processing charge was unavailable.
The manuscript was subsequently adapted and submitted to IEEE Access.

SoftwareX-named plans, preparation documents, build tools and generated-workspace
names remain in the repository for provenance and compatibility. They do not
indicate a completed SoftwareX submission, publication or acceptance. They do
not constitute current IEEE Access submission instructions.

The original historical records and archived releases are retained. Documentation
clarifications do not retroactively change those releases or their evidence.

This clarification reflects the corresponding author's account, confirmed on
20 October 2026. The current revision work is directed toward IEEE Access.
