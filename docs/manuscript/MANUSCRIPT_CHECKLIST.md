# SoftwareX manuscript checklist

## Approved manuscript

- Final editable manuscript: `word/VLab-Orchestrator_SoftwareX_Manuscript.docx`
- Review status: approved by the supervising professor
- Target: SoftwareX Original Software Publication template, version 6 (March 2026)
- Verified layout: 6 pages, 3,440 Word-counted words, 3 figures, 3 tables
- Verified document state: no comments, tracked revisions, or placeholders
- Required sections: Motivation and significance; Software description; Illustrative examples; Impact; Conclusions

## Proposed figure budget

1. Layered framework and adapter architecture.
2. Setup window and backend readiness workflow.
3. Session, queue, authority, and telemetry sequence.
4. Rights-cleared two-user showcase.
5. Five-scenario benchmark protocol.
6. Descriptive cross-backend results with anomaly-validation annotations.

## Completed publication requirements

- Author order, affiliations, ORCIDs, corresponding author, and support email confirmed.
- MIT licensing and third-party redistribution boundaries documented.
- Public GitHub repository and versioned `v1.0.0` release published.
- Release archived at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21756997.
- Required declarations, CRediT roles, funding, AI-use statement, and availability statement included.
- Approved text is in the official Word template with final vector figures.
- Repository contains the final README, licence, guides, protocol, and retained evidence.

## Submission portal actions

- Upload the final editable manuscript and separate figure files.
- Upload the cover letter, Highlights, and Declaration of Interest form if requested.
- Enter author, funding, repository, DOI, and data-availability metadata in Editorial Manager.
- Review the system-generated submission PDF before author approval.

## Comparison policy

- Compare capabilities against related reusable Unity and collaborative-XR software to establish the research gap.
- Compare backend observations only under the declared protocol and hardware.
- Report raw data, uncertainty, effect sizes, and protocol validity when replicated data are available.
- Do not rank a backend as universally best.
- Keep targeted anomaly reruns separate from the balanced experiment.
